LEGAL DOCUMENTATION

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for a membership, submit an enquiry, or interact with FITZONE Gym, we collect basic identifying information including your full name, email address, contact phone number, and selected workout programs. We do not store sensitive payment card details on our local database.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Manage your gym membership access and training schedules.
  • Track workout appointments and booking approvals.
  • Send service notifications, password resets, and account verification emails.
  • Ensure gym safety and compliance with club policies.

3. Data Security & Cloud Storage

Your personal records are stored securely using Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication with encrypted communication protocols (HTTPS/SSL). Only authorized FITZONE administrators have access to customer booking records.

4. Member Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to review your stored profile details at any time via your Member Dashboard. To request complete removal of your data, please contact gym management at contact@fitzonegym.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, visit our fitness center in person or contact us via email at contact@fitzonegym.com.
