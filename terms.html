MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Last updated: November 2026

1. Membership Guidelines

By purchasing or enrolling in any FITZONE membership (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly), you agree to abide by all club rules, safety protocols, and opening hours (6:00 AM – 10:00 PM Monday through Saturday; 7:00 AM – 1:00 PM Sunday). Memberships are personal and non-transferable without prior written approval from gym management.

2. Health & Physical Readiness

Members confirm they are physically capable of engaging in exercise and have consulted a qualified physician if they possess pre-existing medical conditions. FITZONE and its certified trainers provide guidance, but members assume personal responsibility for working within their physical limitations.

3. Gym Etiquette & Facility Use

  • Proper athletic footwear and workout attire are mandatory at all times.
  • Always re-rack dumbbells, weight plates, and bars after completion.
  • Wipe down machine touchpoints and benches after use using available sanitizers.
  • Respect fellow members and staff. Harassment or disruptive behavior will result in immediate termination of membership.

4. Bookings & Inquiries

Online bookings and program requests submitted through the FITZONE website are subject to availability and formal confirmation by gym administrators. A booking submission does not guarantee admission until approved on the Member Dashboard.

5. Amendments

FITZONE reserves the right to modify operating hours, equipment availability, and membership rules with advance notice posted inside the facility and on the official website.
